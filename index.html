About · 缘起

Things that hold a story.

We traverse across mountains and factories of China, interviewed artisans and independent makers, and found beautiful objects yet to be discovered in the world.

Chestnut Eave is a curation of China's finest craft — solid wood furniture meant to last generations, cushions made from natural palm fiber, and ceramics from the kilns of Jingdezhen.

It's for those who want their home to hold something meaningful. And we hope that our products make our home, well, feel like home.
