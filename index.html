A blend of the traditional and modern.

The celadon glaze is rooted in Song dynasty aesthetics (宋式美学 sòng shì měi xué). It’s like having a small bit of our history in the kitchen.

Designs from the past is then integrated with modern design and technology.

Craftsmanship passed down from thousand years

Porcelain produced in this region has a high bar for quality and design. They’re fired at around 1300°C, hot enough that the clay itself vitrifies into a dense, glass-like body that resists stains and odors. And even though they look delicate, they don’t chip easily (dishwasher and microwave safe is always a plus).

It's the same technique the kilns of Jingdezhen have been perfecting for a thousand years.
