furniture · 家私

A 'tea sofa', a living
room's gathering place

The "arhat bed" 罗汉床 has sat at the center of a Chinese living room for centuries — part daybed, part gathering place. It's evolved from the couch (ta 榻) of the Han Dynasty, featuring railings on three sides and has a relatively compact frame.

Receive guests, sip tea, meditate — it's the perfect addition to a modern home.

our philosophy · 溯源

We don't sell what we haven't seen made.

We believe in honesty in materials, craftsmanship and sustainability. We visit the workshop, sat down with the founders and makers (over tea), and partner with products we trust. It's a promise we keep to our customers, and ourselves.

from the makers · 遇见就是缘

Voices from the road

"To understand a product from the East, you have to understand the culture behind it. For example, women traditionally wouldn't sit fully back on this stool — it wasn't built for comfort, it was built to keep you upright. That's the story embedded in the object."

真的要了解东方的产品，那还是要先了解东方文化。比如女孩子一般都不会坐满这个凳子，它不是为了让你舒服，它是礼。这就是器物的故事。

孔春梅 @东家家具

"Our secret to the traditional method isn't really a secret. It's care — don't skip steps, give every stage its full measure. Staying true to the tradition is much harder than innovating. Staying true means holding the line on what's authentic."

我们做古法的秘诀，也没什么秘诀，就是用心，不要省步骤，每一步要给足料。守正是比创新要难很多。守正是什么意思？就是守住正统。

周晓茜 @唐贤香云纱
